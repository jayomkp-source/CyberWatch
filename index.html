Critical Incident File #2026-SBI

State Bank of India (SBI) PII & Loan Data Breach

Incident Date: October – November 2026
Status: Under CERT-In Forensic Audit

Overview: Between October 10 and 15, 2026, the country's largest public-sector lender, the State Bank of India (SBI), experienced an unauthorized data exposure incident linked to its peripheral server environments at the Global IT Centre. Threat actors operating under the moniker "Siriusim" exfiltrated approximately 32 CSV files, JSON payloads, and 2,721 PDF files containing sensitive personally identifiable information (PII) totaling nearly 375,000 records.

Exposed Data Points: Following threat intelligence alerts from the Defence Cyber Agency and CERT-In on October 16, SBI's preliminary investigation confirmed that 3,897 records contained verified customer PII. Exposed fields included customer names, registered mobile numbers, email addresses, residential addresses, dates of birth, branch details, and Central KYC numbers alongside loan application metadata.

Impact & Mitigation: SBI immediately activated incident-response protocols and notified stock exchanges on October 21 under SEBI guidelines. The bank confirmed that core banking infrastructure, customer account balances, and transaction funds remained completely secure and unaffected. Affected customers were directly notified via email and WhatsApp, and an official FIR was lodged in Navi Mumbai under the BNS and IT Act invoking charges of criminal conspiracy and data theft.

High Severity Case #2026-COOP

Regional Cooperative Bank & Gold NBFC Ransomware Sieges

Campaign Period: Mid 2026
Status: Remediated / Patched

Overview: Throughout 2026, threat intelligence agencies noted a surge in targeted double-extortion ransomware attacks aiming at tier-2 and tier-3 cooperative banks and regional gold loan NBFCs across western and southern India. Attackers leveraged compromised vendor credentials to deploy customized lockers encrypting core database clusters.

Attack Vector: Initial access was achieved via legacy RDP endpoints left exposed without multi-factor authentication, followed by privilege escalation using living-off-the-land binaries (LotLB). Once inside, threat groups extracted unencrypted loan applicant archives before deploying ransomware payloads.

Resolution: CERT-In issued emergency advisories mandating air-gapped backup verification, egress firewall lockdowns, and strict zero-trust segmentation for peripheral banking applications.

Verified Sources & Disclosures

  • CERT-In Threat Bulletin Advisory on Ransomware Tactics Targeting Financial Cooperatives (2026).
  • Seqrite Threat Labs Quarterly Enterprise Malware & Ransomware Analysis Report (2026).
Medium Severity Case #2025-UPI

UPI Gateway API & Automated Mule Account Syndicates

Campaign Period: 2025 – 2026
Status: Active Enforcement

Overview: Cybercrime syndicates orchestrated large-scale automated fraud operations by exploiting lax rate-limiting on third-party UPI payment application programming interfaces (APIs). Thousands of mule accounts were rented or fraudulently opened to launder proceeds derived from digital arrest and task scams.

Mechanism: Automated bots flooded merchant onboarding portals with synthetic credentials to generate verified virtual payment addresses (VPAs) before executing lightning-fast micro-transactions to disperse funds across multi-tiered shell accounts.

Countermeasures: The National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) alongside law enforcement agencies deployed AI-driven behavioral anomaly detection filters and enforced mandatory device-binding and biometric verification protocols.
